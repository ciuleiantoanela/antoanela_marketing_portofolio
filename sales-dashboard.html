Manager's Insight

December was our strongest month by far — revenue hit nearly 2.3× the January figure, driven by gifting season foot traffic and successful upselling of outerwear alongside tops. January saw a sharp drop as expected post-holiday, which we used as an opportunity to focus on team coaching, process improvement, and restocking routines. Tops consistently led category sales across every month — confirming they should anchor any promotional or visual merchandising strategy.
