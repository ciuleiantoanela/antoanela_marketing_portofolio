Launching Nova Fashion
online — a GTM strategy.

A go-to-market plan for the fictional launch of Nova Fashion's e-commerce store, targeting Romanian working women aged 28–45. Built on firsthand retail observations, real consumer insight, and a sharp understanding of what makes Romanian women trust — and buy from — a fashion brand online.

Brand: Nova Fashion (fictional)
Objective: Online store launch
Market: Romania
Audience: Women 28–45
Budget: €18,000
Timeline: 12 weeks
01 — Situation Analysis

Where Nova Fashion stands.

Nova Fashion is an established Romanian women's fashion brand with a loyal in-store customer base. It has strong brand recognition in Bucharest but zero e-commerce presence. This strategy outlines how to launch online without cannibalising in-store revenue — and without alienating its core customer.

Opportunities
Romanian e-commerce grew 30%+ post-2020 — the core 28–45 segment is now comfortable shopping online
No dominant Romanian fashion brand owns the "corporate elegant" online space
Inclusive sizing and distinctive colour palettes are underserved in local fast fashion
Online channel allows reach beyond Bucharest to Cluj, Timișoara, Iași
Website discovery is already the #1 channel for this demographic — they're ready
Challenges
Core customer is loyal to in-store experience — trust must be rebuilt online
Price parity between online and in-store is critical — inconsistency kills trust
Competition from Zara, H&M, and ASOS with established online infrastructure
Returns and sizing uncertainty are key barriers for first-time online fashion buyers
Low social media presence means brand awareness must be built from scratch online
💡
Retail Insight — from the floor

During my time in fashion retail, one friction point came up repeatedly: the same item was cheaper on the brand's website than on the shop floor. Customers noticed. Some felt cheated for coming in. Others pulled out their phones mid-conversation to check. This single inconsistency eroded trust in both channels simultaneously. For Nova Fashion's launch, price parity across online and in-store is non-negotiable — it is the foundation everything else is built on.

⚖️
Price Parity Policy — a launch requirement, not an afterthought

Nova Fashion will enforce identical pricing across all channels from day one. Online-exclusive promotions (e.g. launch discount codes) will be clearly labelled as temporary and time-limited — never presented as the "real" price. In-store staff will be briefed on the online offer so they can speak to it confidently rather than be caught off guard. Any promotional difference must benefit the customer equally across both channels.

04 — Channel Strategy

Where we show up.

Each channel plays a specific role in the funnel. No channel is used for everything — focus and clarity drive better results than being everywhere at once.

🌐
Website & SEO
Discovery & Conversion
The primary discovery channel for the 28–45 segment. Editorial lookbooks, outfit guides, and size advice pages drive organic traffic and time-on-site. Product pages prioritise fit information and multiple styling options.
35% of budget
📧
Email Marketing
Retention & Re-engagement
Launch waitlist → exclusive early access email. Weekly editorial newsletter with new arrivals, styling tips, and behind-the-scenes. Abandoned basket sequences with no pressure — just a gentle reminder.
15% of budget
📸
Instagram
Brand Building
Aspirational but real. Campaign imagery of Romanian women in workplace and social settings — not studios. Stories used for behind-the-scenes and styling polls. Reels for product reveals and outfit transitions.
20% of budget
🎵
TikTok
Awareness (Secondary)
Not the core channel for this demographic, but used selectively for "how to style" content and behind-the-scenes of the launch. Trend-adjacent without being trend-dependent.
10% of budget
🎯
Meta Ads
Acquisition
Targeted at women 28–45 in major Romanian cities. Lookalike audiences built from in-store loyalty data. Creative focuses on product quality and brand story — not discounts.
15% of budget
🤝
Influencer Partnerships
Trust & Reach
Micro-influencers (5K–50K), Romanian, working women or lifestyle creators. Authenticity over reach — one honest review from a relatable woman beats a mass campaign for this audience.
5% of budget
05 — Launch Timeline

12 weeks to launch.

Weeks 1–3 · Pre-launch
Build anticipation before the doors open
Launch a "coming soon" landing page with waitlist sign-up — early subscribers get 15% off on launch day
Begin Instagram content: brand story, behind-the-scenes of the collection, team introductions
Brief in-store staff on the online launch — they become ambassadors, not competitors
Send PR packages to 5 selected Romanian micro-influencers with embargo date
Confirm price parity policy across all channels and update in-store POS materials
Weeks 4–6 · Launch week
Make noise — then convert
Send launch email to waitlist with exclusive early access link (24h before public)
Go live on website — full collection, lookbook, size guide, and styling editorial
Influencer content goes live simultaneously across platforms
Activate Meta Ads targeting lookalike audiences — launch creative leads with brand story, not product
In-store: QR codes on mirrors and fitting rooms linking to the online store and wishlist feature
Weeks 7–9 · Momentum
Learn fast, optimise faster
Analyse first-purchase data: which categories, which cities, which channels converting best
Launch abandoned basket email sequence for users who browsed but didn't buy
Introduce "style it with" cross-sell recommendations on product pages based on early sales data
Collect and publish first customer reviews — social proof is critical for the 28–45 segment
Weeks 10–12 · Retention
Turn first-time buyers into loyal customers
Launch "Nova Insider" loyalty programme — points for purchases, reviews, and referrals
Send personalised "you might also love" emails based on first purchase category
Run 12-week performance review: CAC, ROAS, conversion rate, return rate, and NPS
Brief next collection campaign based on what sold and what the audience responded to
